PT Pro – Clinical Information Note

Rounded Shoulder Posture

Definition and mechanism of development

Rounded shoulder posture is a postural disorder in which the shoulders deviate forward and the shoulder blades are positioned forward and tilted downward. It generally develops due to prolonged slouched sitting, weak back muscles, and shortening of the chest muscles.

Short/tight muscles: Pectoralis major–minor, latissimus dorsi, upper trapezius, levator scapulae
Weak/lengthened muscles: Lower–middle trapezius, rhomboids, serratus anterior, deep neck flexors

Exercise approach
  • Pectoral stretch: Place the arm against a doorway at a 90° angle and rotate the torso outward
  • Chest opening with a foam roller: Allow the arms to rest comfortably on the floor in the “T” and “Y” positions
  • Y-to-W or L-to-Y movements: For activation of the lower and middle trapezius
  • Scapular retraction–depression: Using a resistance band or TRX
  • Serratus anterior / deep neck flexor activation: “Wall slide” and “chin tuck” exercises
Points to consider
  • Exercises that shorten the chest muscles, such as bench press, dips, and chest fly
  • Close-grip rowing or pressing movements that increase internal rotation
  • Sitting hunched for prolonged periods or using a phone while leaning forward
  • Fast, uncontrolled shoulder rotations or excessive stretching
